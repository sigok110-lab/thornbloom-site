Thornbloom Support

English

Questions, bugs or a purchase that did not arrive? Write to sigok110@gmail.com with your iPhone model and what happened.

Bought something on another iPhone? Open Settings (the speaker button in the garden) > Extras > Restore purchases.

한국어
